About the Author

R. K. Narayan (1906–2001) was one of India's most celebrated English-language writers. His works often portray life in the fictional South Indian town of Malgudi. His major works include Swami and Friends, The Bachelor of Arts, The English Teacher and The Guide.

His prose is known for simplicity, irony, humour and close observation of ordinary human life.

About the Text

An Astrologer's Day is a short story involving irony, fate, deception, suspense and the consequences of the past. The astrologer earns his living through observation, practice and shrewd guesswork rather than genuine astrological knowledge.

Summary

The astrologer appears to be a professional fortune teller, but his success comes mainly from observation, practice and guesswork. One evening he meets Guru Nayak, a stranger who asks whether he will succeed in his search for a man who once attacked him.

The astrologer recognises the situation from his own past. Years earlier, during a quarrel in his village, he had attacked Guru Nayak and believed him dead after pushing his unconscious body into a well. Guru Nayak survived because a passer-by rescued him.

The astrologer tells Guru Nayak that the man he seeks is dead and advises him to return home and never travel south again. Guru Nayak accepts the prediction and leaves. At home, the astrologer reveals that a great burden has been lifted because he had feared that he had killed a man.

Central Ideas

  • Fate and human choice
  • The consequences of past actions
  • Irony and deception
  • Survival and transformation
  • Appearance versus reality
  • Human psychology and observation

Significance of the Title

The title seems to describe an ordinary working day, but that day becomes extraordinary when the astrologer encounters a man connected with his hidden past. The meeting changes his understanding of his own life and releases him from a long-held fear.

Characters

The Astrologer: observant, clever, resourceful and deeply affected by his past.

Guru Nayak: forceful, suspicious and determined to find the man he believes attacked him.
